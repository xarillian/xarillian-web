# xarillian-web

My website: https://xarillian.com

The background is a live drawn pixel art rendition of the bridge in Prince Albert, Saskatchewan. It matches the actual weather, river level, aurora, stars, moon phase, and time of day.

Add `?debug` to the URL for a row of tiles that pin each of these (and a few rarer sights) by hand.

## Data

- Weather, air quality, and the river gauge (05GG001) come from [Environment and Climate Change Canada](https://api.weather.gc.ca/). Contains information licensed under the [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
- The Kp index for the aurora comes from [NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/).
- The stars are the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit & Warren 1991, VizieR V/50).
